The primary focus is short-form paid advertising.

Authentic, native-looking advertisements designed to feel like organic social content while still following a direct-response structure.

Ads built around a clear problem, solution, benefit, proof and CTA.

Voiceover-led advertisements using a combination of talking-head footage, B-roll, product visuals, AI-generated visuals, demonstrations and explanatory animations.

Founderexpert talking directly to camera with supporting B-roll, product visuals and visual explanations.

Product-focused creatives designed around demonstrations, benefits, usage, transformation, problemsolution storytelling and CTA.

### AI-Assisted Ads

Ads where AI-generated images, video, animation, environments, characters, anatomy visuals, product scenes or B-roll are integrated into a professionally edited advertisement.

# 9. HOOK STRATEGY

Hooks are one of the most important parts of the editing workflow.

The website should have a dedicated section explaining that one concept can be turned into multiple opening variations.

Instead of creating only one advertisement, multiple hooks can be developed from the same core body.

### Hook categories include

#### Problem Hook

Immediately identifies a pain point.

#### Curiosity Hook

Creates an information gap that encourages the viewer to continue.

#### Pattern-Interrupt Hook

Uses an unexpected visual or statement.

#### Contrarian Hook

Challenges something the viewer already believes.

#### Demonstration Hook

Starts directly with the productprocessresult.

#### Question Hook

Opens with a question the target audience immediately relates to.

#### Story Hook

Begins in the middle of a personal experience.

#### Transformation Hook

Starts with the result and then explains how it happened.

#### Warning Hook

Creates urgency around a potential mistakeproblem.

#### Discovery Hook

Frames the content as something the speaker recently discovered.

# 12. AD STRUCTURE

The editing workflow commonly follows a structure such as

### 0–3 SEC

HOOK

Stop the scroll.

### 3–8 SEC

PROBLEM  CONTEXT

Identify the viewer's problem or create curiosity.

### 8–20 SEC

EXPLANATION

Explain what is happening and why it matters.

### 20–35 SEC

MECHANISM  SOLUTION

Introduce the solution or explain how it works.

### 35–50 SEC

PROOF  TRANSFORMATION

Show evidence, demonstration, testimonial or result.

### 50–60+ SEC

CTA

Tell the viewer what to do next.

Exact timing can change depending on the script.

The key principle is

Every section needs a visual reason to keep watching.

# 14. VOICEOVER-DRIVEN EDITING

A large part of the workflow is built around the voiceover.

The process

Voiceover → Script Breakdown → Visual Mapping → Timeline → Supporting B-Roll → Final Polish

Each sentence or phrase can be mapped to a specific visual.

Example

VO You keep doing X...

→ Relevant UGC shot.

VO But here's what you're missing...

→ Visual change  pattern interrupt.

VO This is why...

→ AI explanatory animation.

VO Here's what changed...

→ Productdemotransformation visual.

This creates a strong relationship between the audio and visual layers.

# 31. TRANSFORMATION STORYTELLING

When an advertisement includes a transformation narrative, visuals can progress through multiple stages.

Example structure

Day 1 → Day 3 → Day 5 → Day 7 → Day 10 → Day 14

The important principle is that the viewer should feel progression.

Instead of simply adding text such as Day 7, the visual itself should communicate that time has progressed.
